Afternoon Ritual

If you are looking for a leisurely swim and relaxing spa treatment, followed by a delicious afternoon tea whilst tucked up in your robe and slippers overlooking the pool, then this is perfect Spa Day for you. With full use of the Spa facilities, Afternoon tea plus a 50 Minute Treatment, or your choice of two 25 minute treatments, you can simply spend a few glorious hours enjoying some me-time or with family and friends.

MONDAY – 12:15pm - 1:00pm
