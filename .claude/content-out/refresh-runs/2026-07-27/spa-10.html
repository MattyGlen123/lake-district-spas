Complimentary Spa Access with your stay

Relax with a Prosecco at our complimentary spa

Our complimentary spa is unique in that whether you are in the indoor pool or any of our treatment rooms, you have unrivalled views of Windermere and the snow capped ranges beyond. The design includes three spa treatment rooms (including a couples treatment room), a Himalayan Sauna, an aromatic Steam Room, two Vitality Spa Pools and a 35ft indoor pool. Please note: children are only permitted in the pool from 10am to 1pm.

Caudalie Treatments

Our Spa helps to soothe away the stresses of modern day living and reap the benefits of taking the time out with our fabulous CAUDALIE Spa treatments. Our Spa facilities are included in all our treatments (limited to 2 hours) so come prepared to rest, relax and recharge while taking in the stunning views of Windermere and the fells beyond. Please note we have a 48 hour cancellation policy. Please call to book treatments.
